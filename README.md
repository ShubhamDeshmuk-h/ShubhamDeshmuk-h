## ⚡ Who Am I?

> **I build companies, not just code.**

I'm **Shubham Deshmukh** — Founder of **[Affor Technologies](https://github.com/ShubhamDeshmuk-h)**, a software engineer, and an applied researcher at the intersection of **AI, full-stack systems, and real-world product design**.

I've shipped award-winning applications, built products used by real people, and spent countless hours turning research problems into deployable solutions. My work lives at the edge of what's technically possible and what's genuinely useful.

- 🏢 **Founder** — [Affor Technologies](https://github.com/ShubhamDeshmuk-h): building the next generation of intelligent software
- 📄 **Creator** — [**CheersPDF**](https://cheerspdf.com): a privacy-first, browser-based document converter *(no upload, no server, 100% free)*
- 🔬 **Researcher** — applied AI/ML, computer vision, cybersecurity
- 🌍 **Open-Source Contributor** — building for the community, not just the company

## 🧠 The Story Behind the Code

I didn't start coding to get a job. I started because I was frustrated that the tools I needed didn't exist — so I built them.

That frustration became **Affor Technologies**. That itch for privacy became **CheersPDF**. That obsession with edge cases became research papers on cybersecurity ML.

I believe the best engineers aren't the ones who know the most frameworks — they're the ones who **ship things that work for real people**.

If you're building something ambitious, let's talk.
